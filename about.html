About ChainClear

A small library of plain-English, data-driven guides on how crypto and DeFi actually work — mechanics, not predictions.

Most crypto content online is either price speculation or a project trying to sell a token. ChainClear deliberately isn't either. Every guide here breaks down the mechanics behind how a piece of the crypto ecosystem actually functions — how a business model works, how a protocol maintains a guarantee — using publicly available information and general technical knowledge, not a pitch.

Everything here is general education, not financial, investment, or trading advice, and not tied to any exchange, project, or token. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything. For decisions about your own money, do your own research and, where appropriate, consult a licensed financial professional.
